Archiving says what it does. The confirmation lists what is removed (the worktree directory, the agent and its terminals) and what is kept (the branch, the final checkpoint, the thread), and its actions are Archive and remove the worktree or Keep the session. An archived session stays listed under QUIET, its row menu says why fork, unarchive and delete are absent, and the head of its thread says the worktree was removed and the branch and checkpoint are kept.
