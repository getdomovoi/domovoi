The phone draws the Domovoi mark where the v2 design does: beside the Sessions title, paired or
not, and above the wordmark while the app starts and reaches its machines. It is drawn from the
design's assets as vector paths in the brand colour, in the form the brand handoff gives its size:
the full mark, with its mustache, at 28px and above, and the reduced mark below. The design's
working variant for launch is not in the repository, so the full mark stands in. A refused
credential keeps its own sign instead.
