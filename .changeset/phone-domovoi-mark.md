The phone draws the Domovoi mark where the v2 design does: beside the Sessions title, paired or
not, and above the wordmark while the app starts and reaches its machines. It is the reduced mark
from the design's assets, drawn as vector paths in the brand colour; the design's working variant
for launch is not in the repository, so the reduced mark stands in. A refused credential keeps its
own sign instead.
