Organisation policy
A policy is a refusal the daemon enforces on every granted machine. Unlike a rule, no approval, mode or client can permit what it forbids, and the refusal is recorded with the daemon as the actor.

Turning a policy off is not retroactive
It takes effect at the next turn boundary on each machine, and the refusals it already recorded stay in the audit log. A member's machine that is unreachable keeps enforcing the last policy it received.
