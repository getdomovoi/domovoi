DOMOVOI · DESKTOP SESSION STATES
The states a session can be in that nobody drew
Five groups: the built transfer dialog, the six read-only notices, the working plan in its edit state, the prompt delivery note, and the screens a fresh install sees when something is wrong. Dialogs are drawn at 560px, which is the dialog's own maximum. Thread blocks are drawn at 760px, the centred thread lane. Startup screens are drawn in a 900px window, with the content column at its 448px maximum.

NOTE
The transfer dialog in Domovoi Desktop.dc.html and the one in the build are not the same dialog. The built one asks the daemon what the move would carry and prints that answer; the designed one carries a written list. Where the two disagree the built one is drawn here, and the differences are itemised at the foot of the file. The two lists contradict each other on skills and on secrets, so this is a decision for you rather than a redraw.
